About Us

{{ company.name_en }} ({{ company.name_zh }}) is a Taiwan-based fan motor manufacturer specializing in AC and DC brushless fan solutions. We offer both standard products and fully customized OEM / ODM designs tailored to the thermal management needs of industrial, medical, telecom, and automation equipment.

Our product lineup covers AC fans in long and short form factors, DC brushless fans in large, medium, and small sizes, and an optional protective guard mesh compatible with all series. Whether you need a catalog product or a fully custom specification, we are ready to support your project.

We prioritize reliability, on-time delivery, and close technical collaboration. Through our in-house R&D and manufacturing processes, we help customers shorten time-to-market and strengthen product competitiveness.
